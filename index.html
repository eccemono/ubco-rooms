How availability is calculated

Every meeting pattern from the course schedule becomes a dated event using its section’s start and end dates. A room is shown as free when the requested interval does not overlap any event in that room, and the room is inside its listed building hours.

Results first maximise usable free time in the room’s block, then use cushion and building preference as secondary preferences. The ranking reserves about 10 minutes of transition before the next class so you are not studying right up to the door.

Rooms used by any laboratory section are hidden by default; the Advanced toggle can include them. ADM, UCH, and CCS studio rooms are never shown.

Room changes are only suggested when a single room cannot cover the whole window. A 5-minute transfer is included between rooms; walking time is not counted as study time.

What this cannot know

  • Ad-hoc bookings, exams, events, maintenance closures and department-controlled spaces.
  • Whether a door is physically unlocked or a room is restricted to a program.
  • Last-minute timetable changes made after the dataset was collected.

Treat a result as “no scheduled class found in this dataset”, not as a reservation or a guarantee.
